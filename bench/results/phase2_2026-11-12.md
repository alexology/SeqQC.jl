## Observations

- Plain input, ReadLength only: now within 0.1 s of FASTX parsing (quality not read).
- gzip with threads=2: the decompression task overlaps inflate and analysis (Illumina-like −31%).
  On the real file the gain is ~1%: inflating alone takes 12.5 s, so the run is bound by
  single-stream zlib-ng decompression.
- The real file is a multi-member gzip (no BGZF extra field), with members of ~31 MB compressed
  (first two members: 31,646,380 and 31,803,342 bytes). Members can be inflated in parallel once
  their boundaries are known; this is the next optimisation to evaluate (see `docs/src/ROADMAP.md`).
